## Overview

**MATH 124: Vectors, Matrices, and Applications** is an introduction to linear algebra built around vectors, matrices, geometry, computation, and applications in science and engineering.

### Course Staff

The instructors are **Kartik Prasanna** and **Suraj Rampure**. See the [Staff page](../staff/) for contact information.

### Lectures

Lectures meet **Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2:30–4:00 PM in G127 Angell Hall**.

**Attendance is required.** Lectures will be recorded and posted for review and for occasions when students cannot attend, but recordings do not replace regular participation in lecture.

## Course Components

### Lectures and Course Notes

There are **28 lectures** throughout the term. Attendance is required, and you must attend at least **24 lectures** to receive full attendance credit. Lectures will be recorded, but the recordings are intended for review and occasional absences rather than as a replacement for attending class.

Expect lectures to be engaging, with substantial time spent working on activities and discussing approaches. The [course notes](https://notes.math124.org) contain the main written explanations and examples for the course and should be used alongside lecture. The notes do not carry a separate grade.

### Labs

Labs meet on Wednesdays and are run by a **GSI (graduate student TA)** and an **IA (undergraduate student TA)**. During the first half of lab, you will work on a lab-specific worksheet that reviews content from the previous few lectures. During the second half, you will get a head start on the homework due the following week.

Labs are primarily completion-based and contribute **10%** of the course grade. Your lowest two lab scores will be dropped.

### Homework

Weekly homework develops the ideas from lecture and lab in greater depth. Homework may include written mathematics and computational applications. Homeworks are normally due on Mondays; Homework 1 is due on Tuesday because of Labor Day. Homework contributes **20%** of the course grade, and your lowest two homework scores will be dropped.

### Quizzes

There are five quizzes, **Quiz 0 through Quiz 4**. The lowest two quiz scores will be dropped, so the best three quizzes determine the **12%** quiz component of the course grade.

Quizzes are administered on a computer in the **Electronic Testing Facility (ETF) on North Campus**. They emphasize calculational-style questions and are completed individually.

Each quiz has a one-week Monday–Friday testing window. Reservations open one week before the testing window begins. For example, Quiz 2 must be taken between **Monday, October 12 and Friday, October 16**, and reservations open **Monday, October 5**.

Each quiz will have a practice quiz on PrairieLearn. You may take the practice quiz as many times as you would like.

### Exams

The course has two in-person, on-paper midterms and one cumulative final exam.

| Exam | Date | Weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Midterm 1 | Monday, October 5 | 15% |
| Midterm 2 | Monday, November 16 | 15% |
| Final Exam | Friday, December 18 | 20% |

The two midterms are not cumulative. Exam times, locations, and detailed policies will be announced later.

### Course Improvement

MATH 124 is a new course. As a result, we will administer more surveys than you might normally receive in a course so that we can learn from your experience and improve future offerings. These surveys collectively contribute **3%** of the course grade. Each survey will tell you how much of this 3% it is worth.

## Accessibility and Student Support

We want all students to be able to participate fully in MATH 124. If you encounter an accessibility problem in the website, notes, videos, assignments, or other course materials, please contact the course staff. Students seeking disability-related accommodations should work with [Services for Students with Disabilities](https://ssd.umich.edu) and contact the instructors as early as possible.

Additional policies covering collaboration, late work, accommodations, academic integrity, regrades, and communication will be added before the term begins.
