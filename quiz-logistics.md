# 📝 Quiz Logistics

There are five quizzes this semester.

- **Quiz 0:** Open from **September 8 to September 11**. Scheduling opens on **August 31**.
- **Quiz 1:** Open from **September 21 to September 25**. Scheduling opens on **September 14**.
- **Quiz 2:** Open from **October 12 to October 16**. Scheduling opens on **October 5**.
- **Quiz 3:** Open from **November 2 to November 6**. Scheduling opens on **October 26**.
- **Quiz 4:** Open from **November 30 to December 4**. Scheduling opens on **November 23**.

Each quiz is **15 minutes long** and is administered in person in the College of Engineering's [Engineering Testing Facility (ETF)](https://etf.engin.umich.edu/). Check the ETF website and your PrairieTest reservation for the current facility locations and the exact room for your session.

## Scheduling a Quiz

You must reserve a time for each quiz through [PrairieTest](https://us.prairietest.com/). To make a reservation:

1. Log in to PrairieTest and select **University of Michigan**. The first time you log in, accept the invitation to join Math 124.
2. Find the quiz you want to schedule. PrairieTest may label every assessment as an “exam,” including our quizzes.
3. Click **Make a reservation**, choose an available session, and click **Reserve this session**. Remember to allow enough time to travel to the ETF.
4. Confirm the date, time, and location shown in your reservation, then add the reservation to your calendar.

Reservations can be made after scheduling opens and until 15 minutes before an available session. However, spots are not guaranteed if you wait until the end of the quiz window. We strongly recommend scheduling in the first half of the window so that you have time to reschedule if something goes wrong.

To change a reservation, open it in PrairieTest and select **Change or delete this reservation**. Changes can be made until 30 minutes before the session begins.

## Before You Arrive

- Double-check your reservation for the correct ETF location and room.
- Arrive **at least 15 minutes early**. Arriving late does not give you extra quiz time.
- Bring your **M-Card**, a **pen or pencil**, and the device you use for **Duo two-factor authentication**. Make sure the Duo app is installed before you arrive.
- Leave enough time for travel, check-in, and logging in to the testing computer.

## Checking In and Taking the Quiz

ETF staff will check your M-Card, assign you a computer station, and direct you to store your backpack, coat, and other personal items. You may take the scratch paper provided by the facility to your station.

Use your device to complete Duo authentication, then place it in the designated bin before the quiz begins. Do not handle it again until you have finished the quiz. At your assigned computer, follow the on-screen instructions to open PrairieTest, select your Math 124 quiz, and start the quiz in PrairieLearn.

Once you begin, you will have 15 minutes. You may answer questions in any order, but make sure to save each answer. Before finishing, check the assessment overview to confirm that every answer was saved. When you are done, finish the assessment, log out of PrairieLearn and the computer, return or recycle scratch paper as directed, and collect your belongings.

## What Is Allowed

- You may use the scratch paper supplied by the ETF and a pen or pencil.
- You may use only the resources made available to you within the quiz itself.
- Personal notes, books, websites, calculators, phones, watches, and other personal electronics are not allowed unless the course staff or ETF explicitly says otherwise.
- Collaboration or communication with anyone else during the quiz is prohibited.
- ETF staff can help with check-in or technical problems, but they cannot answer questions about quiz content.

## Accommodations

Approved SSD accommodations should appear when you make your PrairieTest reservation. After reserving, open the reservation and verify that any extended-time or reduced-distraction accommodations are listed correctly. If something is missing, contact the course staff and the ETF before your quiz.

If you have an approved accommodation involving breaks, food, drink, medication, or another need, follow the instructions provided by the ETF. Food and drink are otherwise not allowed at the computer station.

## If You Miss a Reservation

If you miss your reservation, go to the ETF with your M-Card and ask a staff member to remove the missed reservation. You can then reserve another available session. A replacement session is not guaranteed, especially near the end of the quiz window, so schedule early whenever possible.
